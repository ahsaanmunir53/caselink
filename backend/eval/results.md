# CaseLink evaluation

Synthetic dataset: 275 reports from 12 operations plus 45 unrelated one-off reports. The data and its answer key are generated by `app/data/synthetic.py` with a fixed seed. I wrote the generator, so these numbers show the pipeline works as designed; they are not a benchmark on real cases.

Pairwise scoring: precision is the share of report pairs put in one case that really belong to one operation; recall is the share of same-operation pairs that ended up together.

| Set-up | Precision | Recall | F1 | Pairs put together |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Rules only (automatic) | 100.0% | 71.2% | 83.2% | 1528 |
| Rules + suggestions scoring ≥ 0.5 accepted | 99.9% | 93.3% | 96.5% | 2006 |
| Rules + every suggestion accepted | 97.2% | 93.3% | 95.2% | 2061 |

Suggestions: 38 (31 correct, 7 wrong). The wrong ones are reports that share a common agency name such as "Prime" or "Falcon"; they are scored low and wait for a person.
